Part D (model answers)

  1. Average cost: total cost divided by quantity produced.
  2. Long-run average cost curve: shows the lowest average cost of producing each output when all factors can change; the envelope of the short-run average cost curves.
  3. Profit maximisation: producing where MC = MR, which gives the largest difference between total revenue and total cost.
  4. Shut-down point: the point where AR falls below AVC (short run) or AC (long run), so the firm should stop producing.
